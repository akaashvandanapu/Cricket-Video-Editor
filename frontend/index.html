Visual checks

Measures hand speed from the player's body pose around each sound. Removes loud moments where nobody played a shot — bat taps, throw-backs, the door.

Tracks a small object travelling to the batsman just before the sound, any colour. Very few false alarms, but on busy footage (a feeder close to the camera, netting thrashing) it sees the ball only about two times in three — so it can drop real deliveries. Adds roughly 3 seconds per delivery.

When the player can't be found around a sound (too small, off-frame, occluded) the checks above have no verdict. By default those moments are left out; tick this to keep them, flagged, so you can judge them yourself.
